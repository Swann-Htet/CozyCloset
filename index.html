How to play
  1. Design clothes in the Design studio: pick a garment, paint it, set a price.
  2. Stock them and make more of your best sellers.
  3. Decorate your shop with furniture or your own pictures. More charm brings more customers.
  4. Open the shop. Customers browse, react in speech bubbles, and buy what they like.
  5. Grow. Earn money, expand the shop, and make your brand yours.

Tip: click objects inside the shop to open things, and press F for full screen.
